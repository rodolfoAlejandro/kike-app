# kike-app
This project is related to a fitness app for Android and Apple own by Enrique Castillo

## Requirements
1. The trainee profile can be either Spanish or English. When they register, the trainee selects the preferred language, how many years of experience, where they train and what equipment they have. With those answers the app will demonstrate which of the programs works best for the trainee.
2. *"Today" view screen* - When opening the app the first thing the trainee see their routines, how much time it takes and a short-time version if the user wants a shorter workout. If the trainee skips a day, it has the option to re-due it where the trainee left of.
3. *Routines with videos* - Each exercise will include a video of the coach demonstrating the movement, the instructions with the preferred language, the series, the repetitions and the a break between series.
4. *Training registration* - The trainee can type in what weight they use and how many repetitions they did. When the trainee repeats the same exercise the screen should show its metrics of the same last exercise in the screen.
5. *Follow-up* - Demonstrate how many session completed and how its metrics and repetitions changed since the trainee started.
6. *Before-and-after pictures* - The trainee can upload photos with the date and compare them. The picture is optional and private.
7. "Testimonies" - If the trainee wants to share their experience, the trainee can send pictures, a comment or both. Before authorizing, the trainee can select to share the profile picture or name. Needs the right to ask permission to share pictures and comments publicly for advertisement in any social media and the ability to retire any future permissions.
8. *Monthly membership* - The trainee should be allowed to register, pay, administer the subscription and accessibility to blocks of training. A starter payment of $29/month depending on the costs.
9. *Coach panel* - The coach account should be allowed to upload videos once and use it in various routines, edit instructions in both languages and publish new programs. The coach should be allowed to view any testimonies permitted by the trainees and download the version that the trainee approved.
